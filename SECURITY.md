# Security

openmp3 runs third-party themes in a sandbox. Its job is to keep a theme away from the network, your files, Tauri APIs and your Spotify credentials. A way around that sandbox, or any other way for a theme or a remote response to reach your account or files, counts as a vulnerability.

**To report a vulnerability**, use GitHub's private reporting: the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't open a public issue. Include the openmp3 version, the steps to reproduce, and a sample `.theme` if one is involved.

Only the latest release gets fixes.
